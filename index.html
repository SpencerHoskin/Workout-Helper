Safety

STOP and get help (call 911) if:

  • Chest pain, pressure, or tightness
  • Unusual shortness of breath
  • Dizziness or light-headedness
  • Palpitations / racing or irregular heartbeat

Bleeding risk

  • Hard hit to the head → get checked, even if you feel fine
  • Black / tarry stools → get help
  • Nosebleed not stopping after ~10–15 min of pressure → get help

Report to doctor

  • Unusual muscle pain — out of proportion to the workout, in muscles you didn't train, or with dark urine

Every session

  • Talk test: you should be able to speak in short sentences
  • Breathe: exhale on the effort — never hold your breath or strain
  • Machines & cables over free weights; collars on barbells; no jumping/drop risk
  • Soreness: no ibuprofen / naproxen (NSAIDs). Ask the pharmacist what's OK.
